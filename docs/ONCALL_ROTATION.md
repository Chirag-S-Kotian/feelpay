# On-call Rotation

Status: Set up.

## Weekly rotation

1. Primary: Backend engineer
2. Secondary: Platform engineer
3. Incident manager: Engineering lead

## Coverage

1. 24x7 pager coverage
2. P1 acknowledgement target: <15 min
3. P2 acknowledgement target: <60 min

## Escalation

1. Primary no-ack in 10 min -> secondary
2. Secondary no-ack in 10 min -> engineering lead
3. Severe incident -> leadership bridge
