# Grievance Policy

Status: Published internal draft.

## Grievance Officer

- Role: Grievance Officer, FeelPay
- Email: grievance@feelpay.in
- Response SLA: acknowledge within 24h, resolve within 7 business days where feasible

## Escalation path

1. Level 1: Support desk triage
2. Level 2: Risk/operations manager
3. Level 3: Compliance and legal lead

## Complaint channels

1. Email: grievance@feelpay.in
2. Merchant dashboard support ticket (when enabled)
