# Merchant Onboarding SLA

Status: Defined.

## SLA targets

1. Signup + account creation: instant (<5 min)
2. KYC initial review: within 1 business day
3. KYC resubmission review: within 1 business day
4. Activation after KYC approval: same day

## Exceptions

1. Incomplete/invalid documents
2. Compliance escalation flags
3. Banking partner verification delays

## Internal ownership

1. Ops: first review and communication
2. Risk/Compliance: flagged cases
3. Engineering: system blockers
