# Privacy Policy (DPDP-Aligned Draft)

Status: Draft for legal review.

## Data we collect

1. Merchant profile and KYC data.
2. Customer transaction metadata.
3. Authentication and security logs.

## Purpose of processing

1. Payment processing and settlement.
2. Fraud prevention and compliance.
3. Operational support and reporting.

## Data minimization

1. Card PAN not stored in plaintext.
2. Sensitive data tokenized/encrypted.

## User rights (DPDP)

1. Right to access data summary.
2. Right to correction and erasure where applicable.
3. Right to grievance redressal.

## Retention

1. Transaction and compliance records retained per legal mandate.
2. Logs retained per security and audit needs.

## Security controls

1. Encryption in transit and at rest.
2. Role-based access control.
3. Audit logging.

## Contact

Use grievance contact in `docs/GRIEVANCE_POLICY.md`.
